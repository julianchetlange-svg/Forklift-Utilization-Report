Acquisition strategy: All new assets are company-owned cash purchases. Operating leases can be exited at any time — no distinction in savings calculations.
Operational view (flag-triggered): Every recommendation is driven by underutilization — a unit below 60% of fleet avg HPD (2.01) triggers action at its store. For each flag we evaluate: (1) in-store consolidation (remove older, higher-CPH unit and let the flagged unit absorb workload), (2) inter-store transfer (move flagged unit to a nearby non-flagged store and retire that store's older/costlier same-type unit), or (3) direct removal of the flagged unit if no better target exists. Conservative filter added: the store where the removal occurs must have overall avg HPD below fleet avg (3.35). This ensures we only right-size at stores that are demonstrably under-utilized as a whole. All recommendations respect the 2×(3K/4K/6K) + 1×(8K/10K) minimum fleet standard.
